# Code of Conduct

Hard-Sphere Bridge is committed to a respectful, technically rigorous, and
welcoming collaboration environment.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence without attacking people.
- Give reproducible technical feedback and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, languages, and identities.
- Credit prior work and other contributors.
- Accept correction when a scientific claim is stronger than its evidence.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or unwanted personal attention;
- publishing another person's private information;
- plagiarism or deliberate misrepresentation of results;
- manipulating benchmarks or suppressing failed validation runs;
- sustained disruption of technical discussion.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and
project-related public communication. Report conduct concerns privately to the
repository maintainer through the contact method on their GitHub profile.
Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject
contributions, or restrict participation when needed.

Security vulnerabilities should follow `SECURITY.md`.
